WAiDrop Support

Need help with WAiDrop? Start here, then email us if you are still stuck.

Contact

Email waidrop@tnkrd.com. Support is provided on a best-effort basis. We do not provide an SLA or guarantee response times. Complaints are handled within the time required by law (typically 14 days for consumer complaints).

If you believe you found a security issue, email us with security in the subject line. Please do not include your encryption key or Dropbox token.

Before you email

  • Check the CLI documentation and MCP setup guide if your question is about reading the archive or connecting an AI.
  • Make sure the WAiDrop extension is enabled and WhatsApp Web is open in the same Chrome profile.
  • If Dropbox uploads are failing, reconnect Dropbox in the extension settings and retry.
  • Keep your encryption key somewhere safe. A lost key cannot be recovered.

What to include

  • The WAiDrop version from chrome://extensions and your Chrome version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • The steps needed to reproduce the issue.
  • The exact error text, plus a screenshot or console log if useful. Redact anything private before sending.

Please do not send

  • Your encryption key.
  • Your Dropbox access or refresh token.
  • Private chats, media or other people's personal data.
  • Passwords or payment details.

Common questions

My archive is not building

WAiDrop archives chat data that WhatsApp Web has already delivered to your browser. Keep WhatsApp Web open, confirm the extension is enabled, and open a few chats so their messages load.

Dropbox uploads are failing

Reconnect Dropbox from the extension settings, check that your Dropbox app folder has space, and retry. If the problem continues, email us with the error text.

I lost my encryption key

Lost keys cannot be recovered. The local archive and Dropbox backup are unreadable without the key. Start a new archive and save the new key securely.

Why are view-once messages not archived?

The published extension never captures view-once ("disappearing") media. Those messages are skipped and only their metadata is archived.

Why are older messages missing?

Messages dated before installation stay local until you enable full-history sync. WhatsApp may also limit how much history it delivers to WhatsApp Web.

WhatsApp changed and capture stopped

WAiDrop works within WhatsApp Web and may break when WhatsApp changes. Updates are provided on a best-effort basis. Check for a newer extension version before reporting the issue.
