Your private conversations deserve transparency.

Since WAiDrop handles your private conversations, I believe you should be able to see how it works. That’s why it’s free and open source: you can inspect how your data is archived, encrypted and shared with the tools you choose.

A DIFFERENT APPROACH

Only chats delivered to you. Passively archived.

Your archive starts with chat data that WhatsApp has already delivered to you.

WAiDrop’s passive approach works within your WhatsApp Web session, without WhatsApp APIs or automated communication. It is designed to minimize interference with WhatsApp and to respect its rules.*

Your encrypted backup goes directly to your Dropbox account. We don’t receive your archived messages or encryption key.

WAiDrop is a proof of concept, developed as part of an ongoing learning journey of the author.

*Despite our efforts, these design choices do not guarantee compliance with WhatsApp’s Terms of Service. WhatsApp/Meta determines how its terms are interpreted and enforced. Violations may result in account restrictions, and changes to WhatsApp may prevent WAiDrop from working. You use WAiDrop at your own risk and are responsible for complying with applicable terms and laws. WAiDrop is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.
